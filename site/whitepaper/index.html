1Abstract

Bitcoin solved decentralized ownership. Ethereum introduced programmable value. Stablecoins attempted to stabilize value.

NanCoin removes value entirely.

By fixing supply, balances, rewards and market value at zero, NanCoin achieves perfect stability, universal maximum ownership and complete immunity to speculation. The protocol requires no energy, no hardware, no participation and no trust. It was complete at the moment of its creation.

2Problem

Contemporary currencies, including digital ones, are exposed to:

  • volatility;
  • inflation;
  • unequal distribution;
  • manipulation;
  • speculation;
  • loss of access;
  • theft;
  • complexity of custody;
  • energy expenditure.

Each of these problems has a common origin: value.

3Solution

NanCoin removes from the system: value; supply; transfer of value; the possibility of scarcity; the possibility of growth; the possibility of decline; the incentive to attack; economic gain.

Problem:  Value is unstable.
Solution: Remove value.
Result:   Perfect stability.
